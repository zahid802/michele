02 / Positioning shift

From role clarity to narrative clarity.

Your profile should help visitors understand what you build, who it is for, and the idea that ties your ventures together.

Today

CEO / Founder, Azany Group Holdings LLC

Strong on seniority. Light on the specific value, market context and founder thesis behind the role.
Stronger impression

Founder building borderless businesses across commerce, payments and opportunity.

Keep the executive authority. Add the story people need to remember.
03 / Headline directions

Five ways to sharpen the first impression.

Each option keeps your founder status visible while making the business context easier to understand. Use the copy buttons to compare your favorites.

Option 01

Best for leading with the broader mission and the markets your portfolio serves.
Option 02

Strongest when you want the personal founder identity to lead the profile.
Option 04

Option 05
CEO & Founder | Commerce, Fintech & Cross-Border Ventures | Building for Africa + the Caribbean
Direct and keyword-rich while remaining broad enough for a holding-company founder.
04 / Current About

The current copy leaves too much on the table.

It communicates ambition and breadth, but it does not give a visitor a concrete reason to remember what you build or the markets you operate in.

As a creative and passionate professional, I am constantly seeking out new challenges and opportunities to innovate. With a diverse skill set and a passion for bringing unique ideas to life, I have experience in fields ranging from law, Medical & Business. Through my work, I strive to inspire and engage others while pushing the boundaries of what is possible.

The biggest upgrade is specificity: replace broad self-description with a concise founder thesis, the portfolio, the problem space, and a natural invitation to connect.

05 / New About

A founder story built to be remembered.

This version makes your range feel intentional, connects the ventures to one idea, and keeps the tone executive rather than sales-heavy.

I’m Michele A. Swan, CEO and Founder of Azany Group, where I build ventures around one simple idea: access should not be limited by borders. My work spans commerce, payments and digital financial tools, with a focus on the people and businesses navigating life across Africa, the Caribbean and the diaspora. Through ventures including Azany, Africa Vision Card and My Island Visa, I’m focused on making it easier to buy, sell, move and manage money across markets. I’m most interested in building practical infrastructure around real needs, not creating products for the sake of products. I bring together leadership, business building and a bias toward turning ideas into operating companies. I also share lessons from building across markets, creating new ventures and solving the friction that appears when people, money and opportunity move across borders. For partnerships, strategic conversations or opportunities to build together, message me here.
